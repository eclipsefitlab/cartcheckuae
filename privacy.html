Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

What this site collects

This website has no accounts, forms or comments, and I do not ask for your name, email or payment details. The search box runs in your browser and nothing you type is sent to me.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address) as described in GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address.

Links to noon

Deal links go to noon.com. When you click one, noon and its affiliate network may set cookies to record that you came from this site, so that I can receive a commission. Their use of your data is covered by noon's own privacy policy.

Advertising and analytics

At the moment the site shows no third-party ads and uses no analytics. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.
