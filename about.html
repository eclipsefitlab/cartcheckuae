About Cart Check UAE

Cart Check UAE is a small, independent website that shares good deals on noon in the UAE, mostly perfumes and sneakers, together with a cashback code you can use at checkout.

How deals are chosen

I look for products where the current price on noon is clearly below the usual price, then add the link here. When I list a price I show the date I checked it. Prices change often, so the price on noon at checkout is the one that counts.

How the site earns money

Links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through them, noon may pay me a small commission. It costs you nothing extra. I may also show advertising in the future, and I will say so on this page.

Not a shop, and not another store

We do not sell, stock or deliver products. Cart Check UAE is not related to any online store with a similar name, such as checkcarts.com. Our only domain is cartcheckuae.store.

Who is behind it

A Dubai-based individual who likes finding a good price. Cart Check UAE is not owned by, endorsed by or officially connected with noon.
